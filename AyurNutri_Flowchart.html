                A3 --> A4[Load Scikit-Learn Model dosha_classifier.pkl]:::logic
                A4 --> A5[Run inference: model.predict_proba]:::logic
                A5 --> A6[Return Deterministic Dosha Probabilities]:::logic
            end

            subgraph "Algorithmic Contribution B: Meal Optimization (Constraint Satisfaction)"
                B1[Input: Target Macros & Predicted Dosha]:::logic --> B2[Load Nutritional Database]:::db
                B2 --> B3[Apply Ayurvedic Constraint:
Filter out ingredients that aggravate Dosha]:::logic
                B3 --> B4[Apply Macro Constraint:
Greedy selection to hit exact Protein/Carb/Fat targets]:::logic
                B4 --> B5[Mathematically Verified Ingredient List Generated]:::logic
                B5 -.-> B6[NLP Formatter converts raw list into readable recipe format]:::ai
            end

            subgraph "Algorithmic Contribution C: Medical Chatbot (Retrieval Augmented Generation)"
                C1[User asks health question]:::logic --> C2[TF-IDF Vectorizer processes query]:::logic
                C2 --> C3[Calculate Cosine Similarity against Corpus]:::logic
                C3 --> C4[Retrieve top 3 most relevant paragraphs]:::db
                C4 --> C5[Construct strict prompt embedding retrieved text]:::logic
                C5 -.-> C6[NLP Formatter answers strictly using context provided]:::ai
            end
